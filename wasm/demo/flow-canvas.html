Find and replace Markdown source

Literal source text, including Markdown syntax; not rendered reading text. Ctrl/Cmd+F in the editor focuses Find. Enter finds the next match; Shift+Enter finds the previous one. Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes; Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z redoes. History keeps up to 100 edits within a 20 MiB payload budget, only for this document. Opening, restoring, or leaving the page clears history.

Source editing does not require the renderer or draft storage.

Local draft recovery

Opt in to save source and filename in this browser after edits. One draft is shared by tabs on this origin; conflicting changes pause autosave rather than overwrite another tab. Images and access grants are never stored. Drafts are unencrypted, may be evicted, and are not a backup. Changes are saved only after a transaction acknowledgment.
